# Pipeline baseline on real footage (2026-11-09)

What the current pipeline (`HEAD` = `a3863e7`) does on a real sponsored product video, measured on
this machine. The source project is under NDA, so this report holds only technical notes. It does
not quote script text, prices or brand claims.

* **Machine:** Ryzen 7 5850U (8C/16T), 30 GB RAM, no usable GPU (`--cpu-only`). ffmpeg/ffprobe
  on PATH. DaVinci Resolve installed but not running, so no Resolve stage was run.
* **Code:** isolated worktree at `a3863e7`. No file under `src/` or `tests/` was changed.
* **Home:** `C:\Users\L14\.media_house_scratch\baseline`. Every log, JSON report and frame
  below sits under `<home>\_qa\`.
* **Wall time used:** about 60 min, from 06:47 to 07:45.

## 1. Summary

* **Ran:** analysis (inspect, audio/transcription, video, content, alignment, story, pacing),
  `edit_plan` and every Layer 4 module (visual_style, graphics, captions, transitions, vfx,
  sound_design). The Layer 5 `timeline` specification also ran. These ran on 4 clips end to end
  and on 3 more clips for transcription only.
* **Never ran successfully:** `timeline_build` failed in **every** run (5 of 5, two format
  profiles). So `audio_mix`, `resolve_execute` and `deliver` never ran. No mix, no loudness
  measurement of a mix, no rendered video.
* **Biggest gap:** the pipeline works on one asset at a time. A multi-clip video (cold open over
  several clips, then hook, intro and timeline) cannot be assembled through the CLI. No script
  or brief can be given to it either.

### Bottlenecks (all CPU)

1. **Graphics rendering is the slowest stage.** Every graphic is rendered frame by frame through
   node into ProRes 4444, even when it is a still image.
   * Sponsor label for the 84.7 s edit: about 5 min (07:13:45 → 07:18:48), 104 MB, **1 unique
     frame in 300**.
   * End screen: 98 s and 66.7 MB, also 1 unique frame. It was rendered **again for every clip**,
     although its bytes were identical: the library de-duplicated the file (same checksum), but
     the render was not skipped.
   * Rate: about 3.5 s of wall time per second of label. A 7.5 min video would need about
     25–30 min for the sponsor label alone.
2. **Transcription.** It runs at 1.1–1.6× real time on CPU, plus about 50–60 s to load the model
   in each process.
3. **Video analysis.** It runs at 1.6–1.9× real time. Of the 136 s on the 9.6 s clip, entities
   took 31 s and embeddings 29 s, so most of that run was model start-up.
4. **Inspect at depth=full.** About 0.08 s per MB: 71–78 s for a 0.9 GB GoPro file.

**Extrapolation (not measured).** The 20 cam clips hold about 23.3 min of footage (9.8 GB). In
estimated wall time:

| Work | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Transcription | 25–37 min |
| Video analysis | 37–44 min |
| Inspect | about 13 min |

### Root cause of the timeline_build failure

I read the code but did not fix it.

1. `music_and_sound_design/domain/planner.py:451` always adds a `tail` music section after the
   picture, under the end screen. The music tail lasts exactly 20.000 s. The end screen lasts
   599 frames (19.987 s).
2. The compiler's `timebase.duration_frames` is floored to whole frames. For example, on
   wecker_gag_1 the tail ends at 29.5429 s but the timebase is 885 frames = 29.5295 s.
3. `timeline_builder/domain/validation.py:566` treats an audio end more than 1 sample past the
   last frame as an ERROR.

So any timeline with an end screen fails by a fraction of a frame (about 640 samples here).
Neither `--format-profile ranking` nor `generic` avoids it.

## 6. What worked

* Every analysis and planning stage ran on real 1080p29.97 HEVC GoPro footage without crashing.
  The results are cached: re-runs show `reused` / `owner_hit` / `preflight_hit`.
* **Transcripts.** large-v3 with `--language de` gives fluent German, and the forced alignment
  uses the VoxPopuli DE model. The sentences read as plausible German. I cannot give a true
  recognition error rate because there is no verbatim reference; the presenter paraphrases.
* **Sponsored content.** `--sponsored` puts a "WERBUNG" label over the full length.
* **Captions.** Disabled by the `ranking` profile. That matches the brief ("no burned-in
  subtitles").
* **Format.** 16:9 at 1920×1080 is honoured.
* **Sound design.** It picks licensed library music (CC-BY) and writes the attribution text.
* **Edit plan.** It is well documented: decisions with evidence, checks, limitations, word-aligned
  cut points.
* **Clips without speech.** They take a "no-speech path" instead of failing.

## 7. Quality problems (with evidence)

Frames are under `<home>\_qa\frames\`. Plan documents are under
`<home>\data\media\other\...`; the asset ids are in the `_qa/*.json` reports.

1. **The edit does not cut like a creator video.**
   * talking_1: 122.9 s became 84.7 s in 2 clips, which is **1 cut** (0.7 per min).
   * talking_2: 1 clip, **0 cuts** in 35.6 s.
   * The brief wants 10–14 cut events per minute and punch-ins of 105–115 %.
   * The edit plan kept a 16.98 s silence (talking_1, source 55.3 s) and a 4.74 s silence
     (talking_2, source 28.3 s) as a "meaningful pause". It also kept a one-word false start in
     talking_1 (source about 11 s).
   * The vfx plan is `no_effect` on every clip: no punch-ins, no reframing.
   * Evidence: the edit plans `fbd171d3…json` (talking_1) and `b9c21bbc…json` (talking_2).
2. **Text cards show meaningless content at the wrong time.** The graphics brief turns stated
   figures into "explainer_graphic" text panels.
   * talking_1 shows a bare day-of-month token with no month at timeline 39.9–43.1 s. The word is
     spoken at about 83 s, so the card appears **43 s early**. The time comes from a coverage
     rule ("59.1 s without a change of picture"), not from the word. Frame:
     `t1_tl41p5_composite.png`.
   * talking_2 shows a day and a product count joined by a comma at 0.13–3.3 s. They are spoken
     at 4.0 s and 14.1 s. Frame: `t2_tl1p5_composite.png`.
   * The cards are small dark pills that sit left of the subject. They have a short in/out
     animation (22 unique frames in 96).
   * The brief asked for a two-lane timeline, a crossed-out alarm clock and a cart counter. None
     of these exists.
3. **Every clip ends like a finished video.** Each one-clip timeline gets a 20 s end screen
   ("subscribe" / "keep watching") and a 20 s music tail. That includes the 9.6 s cold-open clip.
   * Without `fire_plate` the end screen sits over black.
   * It is a static frame: 1 unique frame in 300.
   * A pixel-dissolve overlay (orange and black blocks, 0.6 s) leads into it. The 0.6 s overlay is
     shown in `w1_trans_sheet.png`; the end screen in `w1_gfxB_sheet.png`.
4. **Word timing stretches over pauses.** Every clip has `long_word` warnings of 5–9.8 s: the
   last word of a sentence absorbs the following silence. For example talking_3 has one word of
   9.8 s, and talking_2 has a word of 5.1 s in the middle of a sentence. Cut points and graphic
   timing take these word ends as accurate, while the edit plan reports `word_aligned` precision.
5. **Sponsor label.** It is about 160×22 px at 1080p, top right, about 2 % of the picture height
   (`w1_composite_t3.png`, `t2_tl1p5_composite.png`). It is legible on desktop and borderline on
   a phone.

   `w1_composite_t3.png` overlays the graphic files at their own time 3 s, not at their place on
   the timeline. It shows the label and text styling only.
6. **Audio cannot be judged.** `audio_mix` never ran.
   * The dialogue source (talking_1, the kept range) measures **−11.3 LUFS integrated, true peak
     −0.4 dBFS**: hot GoPro audio with no headroom.
   * The music and effects stem measures −26.9 LUFS (talking_1), with the bed at −12.9 dB from
     0.0 s under the speech.
   * The GoPro files have 2 AAC streams. I did not check which one the pipeline uses.
7. **Content and script mismatch goes unnoticed.** These cases need a person to listen:
   * The time shown on the phone clock and spoken in the S01 voice line differs from the time
     in the script.
   * One date in the S02 hook has a different month from the script (word confidence 0.72),
     either misspoken or misrecognised.

   The pipeline has no script, so it cannot flag either case.
8. **An approval-gated line would be kept.** talking_3, 102.7–109.3 s, contains a line that the
   script marks as only allowed with the sponsor's written approval. The default is to drop it.
   The pipeline has no way to know this.
9. **Colour.** Nothing was graded or corrected. visual_style made a plan only, and vfx
   `no_effect` means the original footage is used as it is.

## 8. Missing capabilities that block a real multi-clip video

* **No project or sequence level.**
  * `--input` accepts several files, but each asset gets its own independent task graph. The
    `w23_audio` run with 2 inputs produced 2 separate reports and 2 story plans.
  * The executors call `PlanStoryCommand((asset_id,), …)` and `PlanEditCommand((asset_id,), …)`
    with **one** asset, although the contracts accept several
    (`media_pipeline/application/executors.py:825`, `:1083`).
  * There is no flag to plan one story or one edit across assets.
* **No script, brief or context input.** No CLI or config key accepts the scripted transcript,
  the scene list, the 3-level context text, or rules such as allowed brands, forbidden
  transitions or approval-gated lines. Story planning is purely extractive from each clip.
* **No still images or graphics as inputs.** `MEDIA_SUFFIXES` covers audio and video only, so the
  packshots (PNG/JPG) and logos (WEBP) cannot be imported through the pipeline. Brand slots go
  through `scratch/import_brand_assets.py`, which I did not try.
* **No B-roll from other assets.** Screen recordings, archive clips and the fireworks plate are
  not used. The `broll` stage was not requested in these runs. The edit plan states: "cover
  candidates come from the alignment's shots only".
* **No final render without Resolve.** With Resolve not running, nothing produces a viewable
  video. `deliver` needs `resolve_execute --resolve-render`.
* **No scene mapping.** Nothing maps files to scenes (no `sNN` prefixes exist), and the pipeline
  cannot infer the mapping from a script.

## 9. Top 10 issues by impact on the finished video

1. **`timeline_build` always fails** because the music tail is not frame-aligned (§5). Every run
   stops before the mix, Resolve and delivery. One fix unblocks Layer 5 and 6.
2. **No multi-clip story or edit.** The video consists of 20+ clips across 17 scenes, and the
   pipeline can only make 20 separate mini-videos.
3. **No script or brief ingestion.** It cannot follow scene order, check numbers or dates, drop
   approval-gated lines, or apply the brand and transition rules. This is a compliance risk for a
   sponsored video.
4. **The edit rhythm is far too slow.** It makes 0–0.7 cuts per minute against a target of 10–14,
   keeps 5–17 s silences as "meaningful", keeps false starts, and adds no punch-ins.
5. **Motion graphics are wrong.** Text cards show context-free number tokens up to 43 s away from
   the word that triggers them. None of the designed graphics from the brief exists.
6. **Every clip gets an end screen and a 20 s music tail**, even the cold open. Combined with
   issue 2, this makes the per-clip output unusable as a segment of a longer video.
7. **Graphics rendering is very slow and not cached across clips.** Still images are rendered
   frame by frame at about 3.5 s of wall time per second. The identical end screen is rendered
   again for every clip.
8. **Word timing is unreliable at sentence ends** (5–9.8 s stretched words). Cuts and graphic
   timing inherit the error.
9. **No audio mix or loudness result.** The source dialogue is already at −11 LUFS with a true
   peak of −0.4 dBFS. The planned music bed sits under the speech from frame 0. No headroom or
   ducking has been checked.
10. **CPU throughput.** About 1.3–1.6 h of analysis for the full footage before any graphics.
    The model loads in every process. Inspect decodes the whole file (about 75 s per 0.9 GB).
    The setup also hit a TLS-proxy failure in `uv sync` (needs `--system-certs`).
